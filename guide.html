New to imposition? No problem. Quire Maker takes the guesswork out of laying pages onto a printed sheet. Follow these steps and you will have a foldable gathering in a couple of minutes.

The idea in one breath

A printer never printed one page at a time. They printed several pages onto one big sheet, folded it, and trimmed the edges — and suddenly the pages were in the right order. Quire Maker shows you exactly which page number lands where, and which way up.

Try it first. The tool already loads with a sample text so you can press Impose & preview and see everything working before you paste your own.

Step by step

  1. Pick a format. Use the Format menu. Folio is one fold (4 pages); each step up folds the sheet again and doubles the pages.
  2. Name the gathering. The Signature letter (usually “A”) is the printer’s label for this quire. Set the type Size and Typeface to taste.
  3. Choose your marks. Tick Print folio marks to show collation marks like A1.1r, and Keep paragraph breaks to treat blank lines as new paragraphs.
  4. Add your text. Type or paste into the Copy text box. Long text flows onto as many gatherings as it needs — nothing is lost.
  5. Impose & preview. Press the gold button. Watch the sheet fold in the theatre, then scroll to Finished pages to read them in order.
  6. Explore the formes. Click any page to spotlight where it sits on both sides of the sheet. Numbers flagged 180° are printed upside down — that is normal, they come right side up once folded.
  7. Print. Hit Print, choose duplex, and flip on the long edge. Fold along the creases, nest the sheets, and sew.

Tips & troubleshooting

My text made lots of blank pages. Text is always rounded up to a whole gathering, so the leftover leaves at the end sit blank — exactly as a real printer would leave them.

Reading the folding theatre. Use Fold and Unfold to move one crease at a time, and Turn over to flip the sheet and see the reverse.

Printing looks off. Make sure your printer dialog is set to A4 landscape with margins at zero, and that duplex flips on the long edge.
